# Hub1z Feature and Entitlement Inventory

Date: 2026-10-05
Scope: Steps 1-5. Analysis only; no application code, database, or deployment changes.

## Executive Summary

Hub1z already has catalog-based feature checks, fixed quota columns, contract snapshots, and paid add-ons. It is not yet a universal entitlement system: catalog kinds are fixed, core workflows commonly have no commercial gate, scheduled services have selective checks, and the existing session-authenticated JSON API is accessible independently of the advertised API/Webhooks add-on.

This inventory contains 99 capability rows, including administration, member workflows, scheduled operations, exports, partial implementations, and roadmap integrations. CRUD actions are grouped only when they share the same commercial gate, data ownership, natural unit, and disable behavior. Constituent actions are named in each row. Seats and locations are excluded as independent entitlement proposals; their existing metering is described only as the requested reference pattern.

Proposed Type and Separability are analysis recommendations, not claims that the implementation already supports that classification. Provider costs are unknown unless the source actually specifies them; customer prices are not provider costs. No current supplier tariff was found.

- `active_contracted_seats()` measures active subscription quantities versus qualifying active allocations; physical inventory is not the SaaS contract counter. These are snapshot-derived measurements, not raw-event averages or exact end-of-day observations. [app/services/platform_pricing.py](app/services/platform_pricing.py#L33), [app/services/operator_billing.py](app/services/operator_billing.py#L443)

- Pending downgrades can restrict new additions before renewal using tighter scheduled allowances. Existing records are not automatically deleted to fit the smaller allowance.
- Billing counts qualifying allocations attached to active seats, while the quota listener counts active allocations directly. The measurement paths are not identical.

## Step 2-3: Classification Method and Legends

### Types and Separability

- **Boolean**: candidate feature on/off permission. A suggested additional meter is not implemented unless stated.
- **Quota**: included allowance/count/volume cap; can coexist with a Boolean gate.
- **Usage**: candidate pay-per-use capability. This is a proposed commercial type, not evidence of existing vendor calls or billing.
- **Core**: Always-on core access/security/control-plane capability that should remain available.
- **Clean**: flag enforcement can be added around identified entry points without redesigning the underlying business algorithm.
- **Refactor**: shared services, screens, financial/credit dependencies, scheduler paths, or multiple data-access paths require coordinated changes.
- **Not separable**: not safely detachable from the stated baseline capability, such as authenticated tenant isolation. This does not mean implementation can never be redesigned.

### Surfaces

`W` web UI and backend HTML/form routes; `J` same-origin JSON/AJAX endpoints; `A` `/api/v1` JSON API; `B` jobs/CLI/cron; `X` CSV/PDF/file export or download; `P` installable PWA using the same web backend; `H` webhook transport. `NG` means no tenant commercial entitlement check. Role/auth/ownership checks are not commercial gates. A dash means no implemented surface was found for that capability.

PWA is not a second native backend: a `W` commercial check also protects the corresponding PWA request. Hiding a tab/button alone does not protect any backend route. No implemented webhook subsystem was found.

### Isolation Risk Codes

These are static-analysis risks/boundaries, not claims of a successful exploit.

- **T**: depends on host/session boundaries and ORM tenant criteria; direct SQL/CLI must explicitly scope. No RLS backstop was found.
- **C**: additionally depends on correct company/user/booking ownership; do not replace owner checks with a commercial flag.
- **K**: jobs/shared services run across tenants and require explicit operator arguments/filters and correct recipient keys. Shared scheduler failures can affect processing of subsequent tenants.
- **F**: submitted foreign user/location IDs are not explicitly revalidated as same-tenant in the cited writer; non-composite FKs do not enforce that invariant. Examples: guest passes, visitors, printing adjustments, locker assignment.
- **S**: shared storage backend/bucket; tenant namespaces and authorization precede download/signing. Quota measurement uses document metadata, not total physical bucket usage; orphan objects/request/egress costs are not a generic usage ledger.
- **M**: nullable platform/tenant rows or platform commercial tables need explicit NULL/operator scoping and platform permission checks.
- **R**: data is aggregated/shared with another capability; turning off its own screen does not hide it from all related reports/services.
- **G**: global catalog/availability and application-wide infrastructure/settings are shared. Global controls are not tenant overrides.

All HTTP rows also share CPU/database/SMTP infrastructure. Login throttling is IP-keyed, not tenant-keyed, and uses process-local memory: one shared IP can encounter cross-tenant throttling, and multiple workers do not provide a consolidated commercial meter. [app/extensions.py](app/extensions.py#L15)

### Data Behavior Codes

- **D0**: no commercial disable mechanism today. Existing data and operations remain reachable subject to normal auth/business state; hypothetical disable behavior is not implemented.
- **D1**: a gated route/export returns 403 when entitlement is absent; source rows are retained. This is hidden/blocked access, not a generic read-only mode.
- **D2**: attendance history remains stored but the ordinary report is clamped to seven days. Export has its own independent gate and can expose older records when separately entitled.
- **D3**: over-quota additions/uploads are blocked, not existing-record deletion or universal read-only access.
- **D4**: a gated email job stops that class of future sends; source rows and deduplication notices remain. Other reminder channels/ungated emails continue.
- **D5**: storage add-on expiry/reduction lowers future upload allowance; existing files remain downloadable under ownership rules, without automatic purge.
- **D6**: White Label expiry can restore branded footers, but an already-mapped custom domain continues resolving; no automatic domain teardown was found.
- **D7**: no implemented integration, so no integration-specific disable/migration policy or provider-created data lifecycle exists.
- **D8**: baseline security/payment/history access must remain available; no license-driven data cleanup or read-only downgrade policy exists.

Business actions are not downgrade policy: invitation revocation can hard-delete unaccepted users/companies, company archival preserves records, lead deletion removes its rows, and invoice/credit/refund lifecycle actions mutate financial state. Do not describe all retained data as immutable.

### Cost Codes

- **I**: no separate billable vendor call identified; CPU/database/network costs exist but no unit tariff/allocation is encoded. Not a claim of zero cost.
- **E**: SMTP email transport; SES-compatible SMTP/config exists, but cost per attempted/delivered email and vendor tariffs are not in code.
- **S**: local/S3/Azure object storage; storage, requests, bandwidth and egress costs are unpriced in repository code.
- **F**: proposed external integration; supplier price, merchant fees and licensing are unknown. Do not substitute public market pricing for code evidence.

## High-Priority Enforcement and Retention Gaps

1. **Fixed kinds constrain packaging.** E2 always entries cannot be tenant-disabled. A FEATURE is not automatically purchasable as ADDON, and an ADDON is not automatically included by `all_features`. G6 requires a built, available ADDON, a paid subscription, positive price and permitted quantity. [app/services/operator_billing.py](app/services/operator_billing.py#L294)
2. **Unknown/new catalog codes are not functional plugins.** The Python catalog is the enforcement vocabulary; DB metadata alone does not wire routes/services or meters. Upcoming USAGE entries have no provider ledger.
3. **Tier booleans and limits are conflated.** `all_features` bypasses many quota limits as well as granting lockable features; there is no generic independent Boolean/limit override record.
4. **API entitlement mismatch.** The five existing session APIs remain usable while API/Webhooks is unavailable. Shared booking service has no tenant bookings/calendar/credits flag, even though owner/admin business authorization is enforced.
5. **Background bypass.** UI/middleware checks cannot stop ungated recurrence, monthly billing, credit cycles, or notification generation. Existing jobs must be part of any future disabled-feature policy.
6. **Virtual-office writer gap.** VO plan editing and NOC PDFs are gated; subscribing to an already-existing VO plan through `_subscribe()` is not gated by the same code. Existing subscriptions continue to be billed.
7. **Retention/export mismatch.** Seven-day attendance history restriction is not applied by the separately gated CSV query. A tenant may retain/export older data without the report-history feature.
8. **Financial shared readers.** Expense pages may be inaccessible while basic financial reports still aggregate Expense data. Payroll pages may be blocked while HR detail still contains salary data. Generic document downloads are independent of the feature that produced the receipt/contract.
9. **White Label is not domain revocation.** New operator-set custom domain assignments are checked, but existing host resolution is not gated on add-on expiry. Footer branding checks do not uniformly cover email branding.
10. **Separate email classes.** Operator digests and overdue emails have explicit gates; customer renewal, parcel, public-lead, invitation and password emails do not share one commercial email budget. Renewal emails are not disabled by either digest/payment-reminder flag.
11. **No unit costs established.** Catalog prices/monthly rates and customer room/referral/locker fees are revenue/business settings, not evidence of Hub1z vendor spend. Model request counts also do not establish delivered-message or successful-payment billing.
12. **No universal downgrade policy.** Existing access is a mixture of 403, seven-day filtering, upload blocking, always-readable shared data and ungated jobs. No standard preserve-read/disable-write rule exists.
13. **Foreign reference risk.** Guest/visitor/printing/locker writers accept some referenced IDs without same-operator loading. Operator tags and basic FKs do not establish a cross-table tenant invariant. This is a static code risk, not a demonstrated exploit.
14. **Avoid immutable-history assumptions.** Some invoice line routes mutate existing issued records; invitation revoke hard-deletes pending records; lead deletion cascades activities. Entitlement disable itself does not trigger those actions, but they disprove blanket immutability claims.
15. **Partial feature promises.** Stored waitlist, referral reward, PURCHASED-credit bucket, editable template and gateway method metadata must not be treated as proof of full email dispatch, automatic reward, checkout or processor execution.

### 5B. Resolution Rules and Versioning

1. Authenticate and validate operator/owner/resource scope first. Entitlements never bypass RBAC or tenant isolation.
2. Verify implementation readiness and mandatory safety/emergency policy. An override cannot turn an unbuilt adapter into a product.
3. Resolve the active pinned base offer version and its dated terms; add currently effective paid/included add-on grants. Trial/promotion grants are explicit dated sources.
4. Ordinary Boolean inclusions combine as OR. Numeric allowances combine only according to a metric's declared rule, usually base plus purchased allowance. Do not SUM Boolean grants or add two unrelated units.
5. An approved explicit per-tenant set/deny override wins over ordinary base/add-on grants; mandatory security controls win over all. Reject conflicting same-priority overlapping overrides rather than silently choosing an arbitrary row.
6. Purchased and tier-included e-invoicing/API/white-label use the same key; a bundled benefit never requires purchasing the same add-on again. Extra allowance purchases add only the agreed delta. Charge only the purchased upgrade, not existing included units.
7. Unlimited is an explicit value on each metric. A feature bundle never implies unlimited staff/storage/capacity. Enterprise can include every built feature while retaining 15/1,000 capacities.
8. Future plan-version publication changes only new sales by default. Renewal of an existing contract stays pinned unless the customer opts into new terms or the contract allows a notified scheduled transition.
9. Freeze prices, entitlements, aggregation units, grace and data policies together. Raw historical usage is not re-rated at a new public price.
10. Cache effective decisions by operator + resource scope + binding/grant revision + time boundary. Invalidate on activation/override/expiry and do not use one global tier cache for customer overrides. Keep authoritative write reservations in the database.
11. Commercial limit exhaustion can never disable baseline auth, MFA, tenant isolation, required financial correction/payment/history or safe operation of an existing centre.

### 5C. One Central Decision Function and Call Sites

Proposed interface: `check_entitlement(operator_id, key, action, quantity=0, scope=None, now=None, principal=None, operation_id=None, reserve=False)`.

Return a structured decision containing allowed, reason, source/version/effective dates, allowed action/data mode, limit, used/reserved/projected/remaining, grace/overage, reset/effective-change time and optional reservation ID. Use one resolver/policy implementation; service wrappers may be named require/consume, but must not introduce alternate policy logic.

- Web middleware maps route operations explicitly to entitlement key/action, rather than prefix guesses. UI renders the same decision but hiding is not enforcement.
- Booking service must require booking.create for web, quick book, API and recurring instances; existing booking.cancel/checkin/history use protected existing-obligation policy.
- Credits services must check allocation/grant/spend/cycle execution consistently, with separate existing-ledger read/refund rights.
- Billing/contract services distinguish new invoice generation/configuration/automation from reading, payment, refund/correction and existing contractual processing.
- Storage upload uses atomic remaining bytes; download uses ownership plus retained-data action policy. Account/VO/GSTIN activation updates use transactional gauges.
- API endpoints require the API channel where applicable plus the actual domain-operation entitlement. External credentials bind tenant and scopes; session AJAX used by the PWA is not automatically paid external API usage.
- Every cron/worker iterates an explicit tenant and checks execute before creating new optional work and again before an external provider call. No ambient request context is required. Chunk/commit per tenant so one tenant failure does not silently roll back or stop everyone.
- Exports check both export entitlement and the same data-window/owner policy as their reader. PDF/file history remains accessible as agreed even when new generation is unavailable.
- Notification/email fanout checks source capability and channel budget; mandatory security/financial messages have protected, abuse-limited treatment. Reading already delivered messages remains recipient-scoped.
- PWA uses the same service checks. Old installed clients must not bypass backend licensing; private usage/grant data is not cached by the service worker.
- Webhook subscription/create/delivery/replay checks tenant grants at dispatch time, validates secret/signature/safe endpoint, and reuses operation IDs for retries. Inbound billing/payment webhooks required to reconcile existing liabilities remain reachable and authenticated even after a commercial feature expires.
- Network reporting uses explicit authorized child-operator scopes derived from NetworkMembership, never globally disables tenant filters or trusts submitted tenant IDs. SSO validates the issuer/audience/subject against the selected tenant before resolving local accounts.
- Persistence checks backstop imported/CLI changes for numeric resource caps; admission and resource mutation share one transaction. Native DB tenant relationships/constraints or optional RLS should complement, not replace, application authorization.

### 5D. Metering, Aggregation and Operator/Billing Views

- Resource gauges: current staff/VO-client/GSTIN/storage/locker counts do not reset to zero at a new month. Derive from authoritative records, update atomically on state changes and reconcile periodically. Store snapshots for billing history when needed.
- Cumulative meters: API requests, messages, verifications, provider signatures and optional export budgets receive period buckets. Start a fresh row for the new anchored period; retain prior events/closed buckets instead of destructive counter reset.
- Annual plans can have monthly usage allowance windows; do not automatically multiply annual limits or give one shared twelve-month pack. Capture the contract rule and timezone explicitly; timestamps/events are UTC.
- Admit/reserve before a potentially paid provider operation, commit the defined billable outcome once, release nonbillable failures, and reconcile provider reports. Retries share one logical operation/idempotency key. Cost entries are separate from customer charges and may remain unknown until vendor contracts are supplied.
- For API request billing, define external principal/method/status inclusion; do not bill unauthenticated abuse or normal web/PWA polling by accident. Webhook attempts may have internal cost, but one successfully delivered logical event should not bill multiple times because it retried.
- Storage allowance add-ons can remain monthly purchased 5GB units while byte gauges enforce capacity. GB-month usage billing is a different product and is not implied by that allowance.
- VO recommendation: count distinct active customer parties with VO agreements per operator, not NOC PDFs or subscription quantity. Per-location/legal-registration treatment needs PO confirmation. E-invoicing license counts active seller GST registrations, independently of provider IRN transaction spend.
- Operator usage view: each key's current usage, allowance, remaining, overage/grace, pinned source/version, reset date, resource scope and upcoming changes; show separate creation restrictions and preserved existing-data access.
- Platform view/export: per-operator/resource/period reconciliation with included and purchased allowances, billable excess, price version, quantity/rate/amount/tax references, provider cost if known, invoice ID and duplicate-prevention keys. A rerun produces the same closed-period billing lines.

### 5E. Soft/Hard Limit Recommendations

Recommended default grace is at most seven days, with warnings starting before exhaustion, subject to PO approval. '110%' for discrete objects uses floor(1.10 * allowance); it does not authorize a fourth account on a three-account cap or a second centre on a one-centre cap. Zero allowance has no percentage grace; unlimited has no percentage threshold. Purchased pack/provider-spend limits are not automatically credit facilities.

| Meter/item | At 100% | Above 100% through 110%, within approved grace | Beyond grace/110% | Existing operation/data protection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contracted seats (existing reference only) | Warn, show upgrade/overage quote; allow existing operations | Continue with notified paid overage where contracted, otherwise time-limited grace | Escalate/quote; restrict only new contracted-capacity expansion if policy requires it, not ordinary bookings/check-in at an existing centre | Never switch off a live centre, revoke memberships automatically or stop payments/history |
| Locations (existing reference only) | Warn before adding another; existing centres operate | Whole-number authorized allowance only; no automatic second location from 1.1 | Require purchase/upgrade for creating/activating new centres | Do not deactivate existing centres on a quota/downgrade |

| Active VO clients | Warn before new client; price quote for added unit | Honor existing clients; only permit extra billable activation with consent or explicit temporary grant | Block new client activation beyond paid allowance; do not invalidate existing contracts | Existing-client details/history and legally necessary NOC access need explicit retention terms |

### 5F. Existing Data and Downgrade Policy

Recommended standard: preserve rows, allow authorized existing-record/history access, stop only clearly defined new premium writes/executions, and retain settlement/cancellation/correction capabilities. Premium computed reports/new exports may require a license while source records and already issued legal documents remain accessible. Storage/resource overage restricts growth, never triggers deletion.

- Schedule downgrade at the agreed effective boundary, not when the request is submitted. Simulate future restrictions and surface conflicts in advance.
- Keep existing booking/contract processing safe; decide explicitly whether already contracted recurring work executes after a premium feature ends.
- Retain notifications/read state, issued PDFs, KYC/signed documents, financial balances, support history and audit evidence under retention/security rules.
- Paid external integrations stop new provider spending after grant expiry, but acknowledgments/reconciliation of already initiated financial operations continue.
- Keep a custom domain temporarily resolving for continuity rather than accidental instant shutdown; remove premium branding/presentation under a notified transition policy, with rollback.
- Expired existing trials currently retain Growth access. Do not silently enforce a new expiry rule during migration. New-trial commercial policy and legacy-trial transition require explicit notice/approval.
- Historical version/usage/price records are retained; future changes do not rewrite old invoices or counter semantics.

### 5G. Migration Plan and Existing-Tenant Risks

1. **Freeze inventory and contracts.** Export effective current behavior per operator, paid_terms/pricing_snapshot, current add-ons/expiry/quantity, trial access, inherited limits and negotiations. Include currently ungated reachable actions, not just catalog membership. Keep the export in secured operational storage; do not publish tenant data.
2. **Create immutable legacy offer versions.** Pin each operator to its actual effective existing terms. Preserve null/unlimited and all_features-derived unlimited semantics; do not silently turn legacy Enterprise into finite 15/1,000. Preserve legacy Scale independently from the newly proposed Scale marketing tier.
3. **Seed registry and typed definitions.** Map all 99 capability families and operation IDs; distinguish protected core, independent Boolean grants, quotas and provider meters. Keep unbuilt products unavailable. Translate current add-ons and allowances; do not parse free-text negotiated_features into permissions without staff review.
4. **Add resolver and read-only usage reporting in shadow mode.** Compare old versus new decisions on web/API/job/export paths; record mismatches without blocking customers or charging new usage. Capture no secret/PII payloads in diagnostic events.
5. **Unify gauges safely.** Reconcile staff, storage and VO ownership definitions. Validate the VO versus seat-counter mismatch before changing the existing reference metric. A bug fix or new meter revision may change prospective quantities, but not historical invoice facts silently.
6. **Add atomic reservations/idempotent events before external adapters.** Backfill current resource gauges and start new period events prospectively; do not fabricate historical WhatsApp/API/verification usage from guesses.
7. **Wrap shared domain operations, exports and workers.** Keep old feature helpers as compatibility adapters delegating to the new resolver. Add explicit API/cron coverage and tenant/owner tests. Separate create/execute from protected read/settle actions.
8. **Pilot selected noncritical capabilities.** Start with exports/report generation and new extra-storage writes on consenting test tenants. Then payroll/expenses with retained-history policy, followed by new paid add-on activation. Keep feature-level rollout switches and per-tenant rollback.
9. **Publish new Starter/Growth versions; do not yet sell incomplete Scale/Enterprise bundles.** Gate ready capabilities and allow add-ons on any paid tier. Public price page renders the same immutable offer versions used by admission/billing; remove hard-coded Scale exclusion only when the new SKU is ready.
10. **Implement providers and enterprise domains in separate release gates.** Introduce multi-GSTIN before per-GSTIN e-invoice sales, tenant credentials and billing reconciliation before messaging/checks, and network authorization before franchise reporting. Require provider approval/load/security/operational evidence for launch.
11. **Retire old checks only after parity.** Verify renewals, annual/monthly periods, free promotions, refunds, add-on reductions, late payments, existing over-limit tenants, expired legacy trials, date/timezone boundaries and scheduler retries. Keep old-to-new audit mapping for support and rollback.

## 7. Open Questions for Product Owner (Maximum 10)

1. What exactly is Starter core, and does Growth attendance include CSV/history together? Should lead export and alert digests be Growth or base? Recommendation: publish an explicit feature/action list and keep security/financial history core.
2. Does white-label app mean branded PWA or a native iOS/Android product? How is Branded Web Portal distinct from White Label and existing free branding? Recommendation: sell implemented PWA/web scope only, one shared benefit key for overlapping branding.
3. Is a VO client one legal party per operator, one contract, or one party per location/registration? Recommendation: distinct active legal party per operator unless commercial intent requires another scope; avoid seat double counting.
4. Does per-GSTIN mean active seller registrations licensed each month, per-provider account, or verification/e-invoice transactions? Recommendation: separate registration license from billable IRN/check events; choose tax-routing/legal requirements with the provider/accountant.
5. Which supplier and billable outcome apply to WhatsApp, PAN/GSTIN, e-sign and accounting? Who pays provider/gateway fees, and do failed attempts/retries consume credits? Recommendation: no tariffs/guarantees until contracts; idempotent logical work and documented billable statuses.
6. Are any-tier add-ons available during trials or only active paid contracts, and how do included tier quantities combine with purchases/free grants? Recommendation: any paid tier; explicitly funded trial allocations for cost-bearing services.
7. Approve seven-day/10% grace, rounding, postpaid consent/spend caps and Starter staff definition (active/pending/inactive versus concurrent sessions)? Recommendation: account-based limits; no automatic fourth staff or second centre; never stop a live centre.
8. What precisely is network reporting and a franchise/sub-tenant boundary: same operator's locations or legally separate child operators? Who owns data, credentials, invoices and allowance consumption? Recommendation: explicit membership scopes and independent child billing unless pooling is contracted.
9. What are the Enterprise SSO/SCIM, sandbox data-copy and actual SLA/support/backup/RPO/RTO commitments? Recommendation: choose a limited provable scope and forbid unmasked production copies/external sends by default.
10. How long are existing prices/entitlements grandfathered, what consent is needed for transitions, and what data remains readable/exportable after disable? Recommendation: pin old contract versions, preserve lawful existing records and obligations, schedule notified opt-in changes, retain legacy trial access until policy is agreed.
