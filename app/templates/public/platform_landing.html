02 · What's included

Everything your space needs.

Inventory

Multi-location, built in

Run one site or ten. Location Managers scoped to their own site; workspace-wide Managers for everything.

QR check-in

Members get a scannable QR. Reception verifies with one tap. Guests and visitors pre-registered by their host.

Billing

India-first invoicing

GSTIN, INR, Indian number formats, UPI/NEFT/RTGS/IMPS. Credit notes, refunds, PDF invoice export.

Your brand
