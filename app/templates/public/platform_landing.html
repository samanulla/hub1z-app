Questions

Good to know before you start.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. The {{ trial_days }}-day trial gives you the full platform, and you can decide what to do when it ends.

Is my data kept separate from other operators?

Yes. Every operator's locations, members, invoices and documents are isolated from every other operator, and each operator signs in on its own address.

Does hub1z collect payments for me?

hub1z keeps the books: you record payments received by UPI, bank transfer, card, cash or cheque against each invoice, and publish your payment details for members. It does not hold your money.

Can members see each other's bookings?

Everyone sees when a room is free. Other people's meetings show only as "Blocked", even to the operator, so private meetings stay private.

Can I use my own domain?

Every operator gets a free subdomain straight away. Mapping your own domain is available on request.
